UTC Clock Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Chrome extension

UTC Clock Pro shows clocks and time zones in Chrome. It works offline. The extension has no account, does not show ads, and does not send clock settings to Sushila Creatives. We do not sell personal data.

What stays in the browser

  • Clocks you add, 12/24-hour choice, and whether the toolbar icon shows seconds are stored with Chrome’s local extension storage.
  • The alarms permission updates the toolbar clock. It does not read your calendar or other alarms.

Network

Time is calculated on your device from the computer clock and the time zone you pick.

Children

UTC Clock Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This page will be updated if these data practices change.
