Location Alarm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Package: app.tejas.locationalarm

Location Alarm rings when you arrive near a place you saved. The app has no account and no developer-operated backend. Saved places stay on your phone. We do not sell personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Places you save: name, address text, latitude, longitude, and which picker you used.
  • Alarms: radius, sound, vibrate setting, and whether the alarm is active.
  • Records needed to cancel an old geofence so a deleted alarm does not ring later.

Android backup is disabled. Deleting a place or alarm removes it from the app. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Location

Precise location is used to confirm you are inside the alert area and to offer your current position as a destination. Background location is used so an alarm you turned on can ring when the app is not open. The app does not run a continuous high-frequency location log.

Active alarms are registered with Google Play services geofencing on the device. That service uses your location to decide when to wake the app. It is governed by Google’s settings and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Search and maps

You can enter coordinates without a network. Address search can use the geocoder on the phone. If a Google Maps and Places key is configured in that build, a search sends the text you type to Google, and the map is loaded from Google Maps. Those requests can include your IP address. Sushila Creatives does not receive a copy of the search.

Advertising

Debug builds can show a Google test banner on the idle home screen after a consent prompt. Ads are not shown while you pick a place, while an alarm is monitoring, or while it is ringing. This release does not ship production ad units. If production ads are added, this page will be updated first. Saved places are not sent to an ad network.

Permissions

  • Precise and approximate location — choose the current position and check the alert area.
  • Background location — ring an alarm you already turned on.
  • Notifications, full-screen intent, vibration, and wake lock — show and sound the alarm.
  • Foreground service — play the alarm until you dismiss it or it stops.
  • Boot completed — restore active alarms after restart.
  • Internet and network state — map and place search when you use them, and consent or test ads in a debug build.

Children

Location Alarm does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This page will be updated if these data practices change.
