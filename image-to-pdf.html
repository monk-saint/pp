Image to PDF – Scanner & More — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Package: com.pdfscanner.imagetopdf

Image to PDF turns photos, scans, and screenshots into PDF files on your Android device. PDF creation runs on your phone. Images are not uploaded for conversion. We do not sell personal data.

What stays on your device

The app has no developer-operated backend and no account.

  • Images you select and PDFs you create stay on your device unless you choose to share or save them elsewhere.
  • Recent PDF names and local file references may be stored on your device so you can open exports again.
  • This release does not use a separate developer analytics SDK.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob for native and interstitial ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics according to your consent choices and Google’s Privacy Policy. Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before personalized ads. You can review ad privacy choices from Settings when available.

Network

PDF conversion does not require the internet. Internet access is used for advertising, consent management, and related ad-network requests.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you tap Camera to scan pages.
  • Photos — only images you pick, or images you share into the app.
  • Storage — only when you save or export a PDF through Android’s file picker.
  • Internet and network state — ads and consent.

The app does not request broad access to your entire photo library.

Children

Image to PDF does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This page will be updated before any cloud conversion feature ships.
