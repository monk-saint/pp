Hexland – Build & Trade — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Package: org.playcatanou.twa

This policy describes how the Play Catanou game handles information. It applies to the website at playcatanou.duckdns.org and to the Android app Play Catanou (package org.playcatanou.twa), which is a Trusted Web Activity that displays that website.

What we collect

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to play a multiplayer game. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login.

  • Display name. When you create or join a game you type a short name (up to 5 characters). It is shown to the other players in that game. Do not enter your real name or anything you do not want other players to see.
  • Session cookie. We set one first-party cookie named catan_<ROOMCODE> holding a random token so the server knows which player you are in a particular room. It contains no personal information and is not used to track you across sites.
  • Game state. The board layout, the names in the room, and the moves made are stored on our server so a game survives a page refresh and players can reconnect.

What we do not collect

The app does not use:

  • Advertising, ad identifiers, or ad networks;
  • Analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, or any third-party tracking;
  • Your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files;
  • Your email address, phone number, or any account credentials.

We do not sell or share your information, and we show no ads.

Data stored on your device

Play Catanou is a Progressive Web App. Its service worker caches the app’s own files (styles, images, and game code) in your browser cache so it loads quickly, and remembers a small preference for whether you dismissed the “install app” prompt. Clearing your browser’s site data removes both at any time.

Retention

Rooms are kept only while they are in use and are deleted automatically once they have been idle for 24 hours. We do not build long-term profiles of players.

Third parties

The game runs on a self-hosted server; we use no third-party analytics or advertising services. If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information under Google’s own privacy policy; that is between you and Google. Links out to external sites (for example the project’s GitHub page) open only when you choose them and are governed by those sites’ policies.

Children

The game is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because there are no accounts and no personal details are required, no personal information about a child is stored beyond the chosen display name, which is removed with the room.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.
