Merge Tiles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Package: app.autoapp.tilemerge

Merge Tiles is a puzzle game. Progress is stored on your device. The app has no account and no developer-operated backend. We do not sell personal data.

What stays on your device

Game progress and settings are stored locally with on-device storage. They are not sent to Sushila Creatives.

Advertising

The game can offer an optional rewarded ad for an extra undo after the free undo is used. Rewarded ads are served by Google Mobile Ads. Watching an ad is optional. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics under Google’s Privacy Policy and your consent choices. Game boards are not sent to the ad network.

Children

Merge Tiles is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This page will be updated if these data practices change.
