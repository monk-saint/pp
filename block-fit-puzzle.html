Block Fit Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Package: com.blockfit.puzzlegame

Block Fit Puzzle is a block-fitting game with an Infinite mode and puzzle levels. Progress stays on your device. The game has no account and no developer-operated backend. Gameplay works offline. We do not sell personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Level progress, stars, and best scores.
  • Settings for sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, and block style.
  • A count of failures, used to decide when an interstitial ad may be offered.

Unfinished boards are discarded when you leave a game or return home. This progress is not sent to Sushila Creatives.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob. After the one free Undo in a game, an extra Undo can be earned by watching an optional rewarded ad. An interstitial ad may be requested when you tap Retry after every second eligible failure. If an ad is unavailable, play continues without it. AdMob may process the advertising ID, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics under Google’s Privacy Policy. Game boards, scores, and settings are not sent to the ad network.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — load advertisements. The puzzle does not need them.
  • Vibrate — optional vibration you can turn off in settings.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob when ads are requested.

Children

Block Fit Puzzle is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This page will be updated if these data practices change.
